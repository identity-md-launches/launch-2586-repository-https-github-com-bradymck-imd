## Deviations from the brief and why

1. **Libraries are not under `lib/`.** The brief and SPEC §10.2 place vendored Solidity under
   `lib/`, and audit finding 78899d2e asks for forge-std under `lib/forge-std`. The task rules
   forbid touching `lib/` and `remappings.txt` (a submission that does is rejected), so the BLS
   libraries live in `src/bls/` (they are compiled into the verifier as internal libraries, so
   the launch's bytecode is fully linked) and forge-std in `test/forge-std/`. Moving them to
   `lib/` later is a path change only.
2. **Epoch gate (audit finding f8ef8bff, spec-versus-brief conflict).** The spec requires
   `submit` to reject unauthorized epochs; the brief keeps the registry (Job 3) out of scope and
   speaks of "epoch mismatch", which has no input to mismatch because `E(r)` is derived from `r`.
   Resolution implemented: the verifier takes an immutable `epochAuthority` address and consults
   `IEpochAuthority.isEpochAuthorized(networkId, E(r))` in `submit` for every epoch ≥ 1. Epoch 0
   is authorized by genesis itself: pinning the group key *is* the activation of the genesis
   committee (§6.4, §7.3). `verify` has no gate (§7.3: the pure library verifies without a
   mirror). The constructor does not call the authority, so deployment works before Job 3
   exists; `submit` for epoch ≥ 1 fails closed until the authority has code and returns true.
   The epoch-authority address is therefore a constructor value the requester must supply at
   launch (the Ethereum registry or the chain's `EpochMirror`); it is not an owner and `$owner`
   must not be used for it. If the requester prefers the gate in a Job 3 wrapper instead, the
   change is to drop the argument and the call; the tests `test_RejectWrongNetworkEpochRound`
   cover both the gate and the ungated `verify`.
3. **`submit` returns `bool`.** §8.2 shows `submit` without a return value. The selector is
   unchanged; the boolean lets Job 7's relayer escrow pay the first valid submitter exactly
   once without the verifier storing the submitter (an extra 22,100-gas slot).

## Launch notes for the manifest step

- Contract: `BeaconVerifier`. Arguments in order: `networkId` (bytes32, never zero),
  `genesisTime` (uint64 seconds, never zero), `key` (struct of eight bytes32 words: the
  256-byte EIP-2537 affine G2 group key, `pad64(x.c0) || pad64(x.c1) || pad64(y.c0) || pad64(y.c1)`
  split into 32-byte words; it must be a canonical point of the G2 subgroup or the constructor
  reverts), `epochAuthority` (address, never zero; not an owner).
- No argument is an owner or admin, and none may be `$owner`, `msg.sender` or a stand-in: the
  group key comes from the DKG certificate (Job 4) and the authority from Job 3. Until those
  exist the manifest cannot be filled; that is a requester input, not a value to invent.
- Constants fixed in code (SPEC v1): period 30 s, epoch length 2,880 rounds, message prefix
  `IMD_BEACON_ROUND_V1`, DST `BLS_SIG_BLS12381G1_XMD:SHA-256_SSWU_RO_NUL_`.
